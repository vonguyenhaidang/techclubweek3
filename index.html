Teacher Guide

Tech Club Week 3 — Human Vs AI

What this activity is

Students explore a blocky 3D world and break open glowing Logiscool logo blocks scattered around the map. Each one asks a multiple-choice technology question. Answering correctly grows the Logiscool school building a little more — after 15 correct answers the building is fully built (3 floors!) and the student wins. Mr. Logiscool, an NPC modeled after a real Tech Club instructor, wanders the map sharing random tech trivia along the way.

Controls (share with students)

  • WASD — move, Mouse — look around, Space — jump
  • Left click — break a block (breaking a quiz block opens a question)
  • Right click — place the currently selected block
  • 1–6 or scroll wheel — choose a block type from the hotbar
  • Esc — release the mouse cursor

Suggested facilitation flow

  1. Let students click "Click to Play" and get comfortable moving around for a minute.
  2. Point out the Logiscool school building near spawn — explain it will grow as they answer questions correctly.
  3. Have them explore and break quiz blocks (they glow with the Logiscool logo). Wrong answers show the correct one highlighted, so mistakes are still a learning moment.
  4. Encourage them to find Mr. Logiscool for bonus trivia while they explore.
  5. First to 15 correct answers "wins" and finishes the building — consider a small reward or recognition.
  6. World 2 — the Evil Twin's Lair: once a student has 15 correct, a purple portal opens on the map (press E at it). It loads a second walkable 3D arena with the Evil Twin in the middle and 15 red blocks around it. Walking up to a block and pressing E opens one humans-vs-AI round; guessing the verdict correctly throws that block at the Twin. Fifteen hits ends the fight, and the tally screen is a good place to stop and discuss which column was biggest and why.

⚔️ The Artificial Logiscool Evil Twin

Beyond the portal waits a copy of Logiscool gone wrong — built entirely from data, with no idea what actually makes learning human.

You land in its lair: a dark arena with the Twin hovering dead centre and 15 red blocks scattered across the floor. Walk up to a block, press E, and it asks you one round of humans vs AI — recognizing, remembering, creating, explaining, solving or deciding. Call the verdict right (Human Wins, AI Wins or Better Together) and you hurl that block straight at the Twin. Call it wrong and the block goes dark for a few seconds, then recharges so you can try again.

Land all 15 hits and the Twin dissolves — leaving behind a Human-Shaped Statue.
